**ASC Default** applies [Microsoft’s security benchmark](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/defender-for-cloud/policy-reference) through Defender for Cloud.

> **208 auditing · 16 disabled · no blocking or automatic fixes.**

| Default effect | Checks | What happens |
|---|---:|---|
| [Audit](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/concepts/effect-audit) | 99 | Reports an insecure setting. |
| [AuditIfNotExists](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/concepts/effect-audit-if-not-exists) | 109 | Reports a missing required resource or configuration. |
| [Disabled](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/concepts/effect-disabled) | 16 | Skips the check. |

- **Why added?** Defender for Cloud assigns it for [security assessment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/defender-for-cloud/zero-trust#zero-trust-and-defender-for-cloud). Creation records identify who triggered onboarding.
- **Paid plan?** This assignment alone doesn’t prove one is enabled. [Plans](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/defender-for-cloud/concept-cloud-security-posture-management#cspm-plans)
- **Policy / initiative / assignment:** One check / a bundle / its scope and settings. [Terms](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/overview#azure-policy-objects)
- **Enforcement `Default`:** Runs the chosen effect. Audit still reports; it doesn’t become Deny. [Settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/concepts/assignment-structure#enforcement-mode)

**Your assignment may differ:** check scope, versions, parameters, overrides, and exemptions. These are version **57.59.0** defaults, checked **5 September 2026**, not live compliance results.
