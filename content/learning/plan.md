This is one ongoing note for my learning plans. I’ll update it as I choose a certification, work through the material, and find out what helps me learn.

## How I plan to learn

My starting approach is to work through a topic in small steps:

1. **Start with a question.** Decide what I want to understand or be able to do.
2. **Read the official material.** Capture the main idea in my own words.
3. **Try it in a small lab.** Build something, change a setting, and check what actually happens.
4. **Explain the result.** Record the steps, mistakes, and reasons behind the solution.
5. **Revisit what is unclear.** Repeat the task without following the walkthrough and note where I get stuck.

I’ll keep useful technical explanations in my [articles](/articles/) and use this note to discuss the learning process itself.

## Preparation steps

Once I choose a certification, I’ll build its plan here:

1. **Check the current requirements.** Save the official certification page and exam study guide, including any prerequisites.
2. **Map the topics.** Mark each exam objective as familiar, needs practice, or new.
3. **Plan small study sessions.** Give each session one topic and a practical outcome.
4. **Study and practise.** Read, do a relevant lab, and explain the decisions involved.
5. **Keep a mistake log.** Write down what went wrong, why, and how to recognise the same problem again.
6. **Check understanding.** Use official practice material where available and review the reasons behind incorrect answers.
7. **Review readiness.** Revisit weak areas and decide on an exam date when the preparation supports it.
8. **Reflect afterwards.** Record what worked, what needs more practice, and the next learning goal.
